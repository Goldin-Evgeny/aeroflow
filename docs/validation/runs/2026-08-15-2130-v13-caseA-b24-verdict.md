## 5. Anomalies

### 5.1 The previous INCONCLUSIVE was two windows short

Windows 1–9 reproduce exactly. The case needed 11 windows, and the 25-minute budget allowed 9. The old budget was not merely tight — it stopped in a region where `q` had already been
flat at 0.659 for two consecutive windows, so the run _looked_ settled while the steadiness
statistic had not yet fallen below its threshold. Recorded because "q looks converged" and
"the steadiness predicate fired" are different claims and this run separates them.

### 5.2 The failure has moved from the ground plane to the building-height planes

Against the b=16 recording ([2026-08-15-1430](2026-08-15-1430-v13-caseA-b16-recording.md)):

| z (m)  | q at b=16 | q at b=24 |
| ------ | --------- | --------- |
| 0.0100 | 0.323     | **0.646** |
| 0.1600 | 0.200     | 0.200     |
| 0.1700 | 0.111     | 0.333     |
| 0.2200 | 1.000     | 1.000     |
| 0.2800 | 1.000     | 1.000     |

The pedestrian plane at z = 10 mm — 65 of 126 points, over half the scored set — **doubles**
from 0.323 to 0.646 with resolution, which is the resolution-convergence story behaving as
the acceptance ledger says it should.

What remains is different in kind: z = 0.16 m **is exactly the building height H**, and
z = 0.17 m sits just above it. Those two planes score 0.200 and 0.333 while everything at
z ≥ 0.22 scores 1.000. The residual error is concentrated at roof height and immediately
above it, not near the ground.

Recorded as an observation. Competing readings not separated here: a roof-edge separation
that the uniform grid under-resolves; the LES closure at the shear layer; or the probe
plane's proximity to the geometric discontinuity. Nothing in this run distinguishes them.

### 5.3 The margin is one point at 1.5% over tolerance

The six closest misses are 0.2538, 0.2548, 0.2572, 0.2577, 0.2593, 0.2641 against a 0.25
allowance. A single point moving by 0.004 flips the verdict.

This does **not** license treating the case as "nearly passing" — 83/126 is the measurement
and the gate is 84. It does mean the verdict is fragile with respect to anything that
perturbs the field slightly, and that any future comparison of this case must be per-point
rather than on `q` alone.

### 5.4 `NUMERICAL_HEALTH` is unscoreable here

The AIJ harness reports no non-finite count, mass drift, or ledger closure, so a whole
verdict axis cannot be filled for any V12–V15 run. Recorded as an `EXECUTION`-adjacent gap
per the run-history rule rather than scored green by assumption.
