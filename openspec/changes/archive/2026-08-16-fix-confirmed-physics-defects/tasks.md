  **Experiment 1 — normalization.** The gate compares an absolute momentum drift against an absolute `5e-5` bar on a harness whose `|p| = 3.2`, so the bar is implicitly `1.5625e-5` **relative**. Stating it relatively is the instrument repair; choosing the relative bar's _value_ is a separate decision and is **not** made here (moving it would be moving a criterion, hard rule 3).

  | λ   | abs drift | rel drift (÷3.2) | vs abs 5e-5 |
  | --- | --------- | ---------------- | ----------- |
  | 3.2 | 4.491e-5  | 1.403e-5         | PASS        |
  | 8   | 4.827e-5  | 1.509e-5         | PASS        |
  | 16  | 7.697e-5  | 2.405e-5         | **FAIL**    |

  **Experiment 2 — step scaling. This falsifies the explanation `PHYSICS.md` currently offers.** Fitting `drift ∝ step^k` over steps 10–120:

  | λ   | k         | R²    | drift@60 | drift@120 | ratio 120/60 |
  | --- | --------- | ----- | -------- | --------- | ------------ |
  | 3.2 | 0.855     | 0.993 | 2.52e-5  | 4.49e-5   | 1.785        |
  | 8   | 0.702     | 0.948 | 2.67e-5  | 4.83e-5   | 1.809        |
  | 16  | **1.247** | 0.909 | 1.73e-5  | 7.70e-5   | **4.453**    |

  λ=3.2 and λ=8 grow **sub-linearly** (k ≈ 0.70–0.86, between random-walk 0.5 and linear 1.0) — the signature of accumulated f32 rounding. λ=16 grows **superlinearly**: doubling the steps 60→120 multiplies the drift by **4.45**, where linear accumulation gives 2.0 and rounding gives ~1.4. That ratio is fit-free and robust to the fit window.

  `PHYSICS.md:191-194` attributes the λ=16 miss to "f32 reconstruction summing mirror directions in different orders", i.e. rounding — which predicts k ≤ 1. **The measurement says otherwise at λ=16**, and superlinear growth is what a weakly unstable mode does, not what rounding does. Streaming contributes exactly zero at every λ (it is a permutation), so collision carries all of it.

  Caveats, stated rather than buried: R² = 0.909 at λ=16 is the weakest of the three fits, so `k` itself carries real uncertainty; 120 steps is barely one decade; one realization only; and the source artifact is dated 2026-08-14, though no Q27 code has changed since. **Not established:** which mode, or whether it saturates. **`PHYSICS.md`'s stated mechanism should be corrected — proposed, not applied, since it is a normative physics document.**
